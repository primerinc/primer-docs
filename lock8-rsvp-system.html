The plan

Three touchpoints, in order

Each email leads directly into the next step, and every RSVP link is personalized to the guest — no logins, no generic form.

01
Save the Date
A short, branded announcement to everyone on the list — just the date and the promise of details to come.
Target: ASAP
02
RSVP & Details
Full event details — address, time, dress code — plus a personalized RSVP form: attending, guest count, food preference, and a few questions from the Lock 8 team.
Target: early–mid September
03
Reminder
A friendly nudge to confirmed — and not-yet-responded — guests as the event approaches.
Target: October 10–12

Before we start

What we need from Lock 8

Guest list
Names and emails for the ~60–70 invitees, and whether each invite includes a +1.
A sending mailbox
Which Lock 8 address should the emails come from — and one click to authorize it.
Event details
Exact address, start time, dress code, and parking info.
The RSVP questions
What, beyond food preference, should the form ask?
Catering choices
Real menu options for the food-preference field, or free-text only.
Sheet access
Who at Lock 8 should see live RSVP and dietary responses.
