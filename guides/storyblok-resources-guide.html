Adding and editing resources

Resources are the posts on your site: success stories, white papers, blog posts, partner pages, job listings and the like. This sheet covers the everyday jobs: add a new one, change an existing one, and take one down.

One story = one page

Each resource is a story in Storyblok's Content tab, kept in your resources folder (for example posts).

Listings update themselves

Archive pages pick posts up by their Resource type, so publishing a post adds it to its list. No list to edit by hand.

Save is a draft, Publish is live

Nothing reaches visitors until you press Publish. Changes show on the site within a minute or two.

01

Add or edit a resource

Add a new resource

  1. In Content, open your resources folder and find a post of the same type as the new one.
  2. Select it and choose Duplicate from the toolbar. Starting from a copy keeps the layout and sections the design expects.
  3. Open the copy, and in Config on the right give it a name and a slug, the last part of the web address: lowercase, words joined by hyphens.
  4. Open it and replace the text, images and card fields (see below).
  5. Press Save to check it in the preview, then Publish.

Edit an existing resource

  1. Open the post from the resources folder, or search for it by name.
  2. Click any section in the preview. Its fields open in the panel on the right.
  3. Change the text, swap an image, or drag sections up and down to reorder them. Use + between sections to add one.
  4. Press Save, check the preview, then Publish. To publish later, choose Schedule publishing from More options next to Publish.
02

The fields that matter

FieldWhat it does
TitleThe name shown on cards and in listings. The headline on the page itself comes from the first section.
Resource typeWhich listing the post appears in (Success Stories, White Papers, Blog…). Change it to move a post to another section.
FeaturedTick it to show the post as the large card at the top of its listing. The newest featured post wins.
Card imageThe photo on the post's card. Use a landscape image at least 1200px wide. If you leave it empty, the first image on the page is used.
Card excerptOne or two sentences for the card. If you leave it empty, the first text on the page is used.
BodyThe sections that make up the page: the header, then the article, then extras like a call to action. The article section has its own rich-text editor for headings, lists, quotes, images and tables.
SEOThe search-result title and description, and the image shown when the link is shared. Optional: sensible defaults are filled in from the page.
Content gateAsks for a name and email before the rest of the post shows (white papers, for example). Tick it on, and choose how many sections stay readable above the form.
03

Take down or roll back

Unpublish

Takes the post off the site and out of every listing, but keeps it in Storyblok so you can publish it again later. It's under More options next to Publish.

Restore an earlier version

Open the post's History to see every saved and published version. Select one, choose Restore, then publish.

Delete

Removes the story, and links to it stop working. It's permanent unless your space has Storyblok's Trash bin app. Unpublish first, and delete only when you're sure.

04

Good habits

  • Duplicate rather than start blank. A copy of a post of the same type already has the right sections in the right order.
  • Keep the slug once it's live. Changing it changes the web address, so old links and search results break. Ask us to add a redirect if you must.
  • Add alt text to every image. One plain sentence saying what the image shows, for screen readers and search.
  • Posts list newest first, by the date they were first published. Republishing an old post doesn't move it to the top.
  • Leave the folder as it is. Moving a post into another folder changes its address. Keep every resource in the resources folder.
  • Check the preview before you publish. Look at it on phones too: switch the preview to Mobile in the top toolbar.

Before launch: while a site is still being built, its preview address may show saved drafts as well as published posts. From launch day on, visitors only ever see what's published.
